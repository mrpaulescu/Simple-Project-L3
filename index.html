01 / SUCEAVA & BUCOVINA

Where Medieval Heritage Meets Modern Tech

Located on the Suceava Plateau in northeastern Romania, Suceava blends 14th-century Moldavian fortresses and UNESCO monasteries with an energetic university community.

Former Seat of Moldavia

Suceava served as the capital of the Principality of Moldavia for nearly two centuries. Today, landmarks like Cetatea de Scaun, Parcul Şipote, and Mănăstirea „Sfântul Ioan cel Nou” sit minutes away from bustling student cafés on Bulevardul 1 Mai.

Suceava Quick Guide

What makes Suceava unique for students?

Everything is walkable: lecture halls, dorms, the Planetarium, Areni Park, and the city center are all clustered closely, while the Carpathian foothills begin less than an hour west by train or bus.

What is FIESC at USV?

FIESC is the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (Facultatea de Inginerie Electrică şi Ştiința Calculatoarelor), home to Suceava’s Computers (CTI), Automation, and Applied Informatics programs.

How is the climate in Suceava?

Suceava has a temperate-continental climate with crisp, sunny autumns, snowy winters shaped by the Eastern Carpathians, and mild spring mornings ideal for outdoor photography.

What is a typical day for Andrei at USV?

8:00 AM walk from Cămin C3 past the Suceava Planetarium to Corp E for a Networks lecture, lunch near Areni, two hours of hands-on debugging in Corp C, and an evening sunset run up toward Cetatea de Scaun.

What is Hard&Soft Suceava?

It is USV’s well-known international student contest hosted by FIESC, where teams design and build working hardware-and-software prototypes under tight deadlines—exactly the kind of challenge Andrei trains for.

How does his CS coursework connect to this webpage?

Built at the end of the 14th century under Voivode Petru I Muşat and strengthened by Ştefan cel Mare, the fortress sits on a plateau roughly 70 meters above the Suceava River meadow.

Mănăstirea „Sfântul Ioan cel Nou” — Str. Ion Vodă Viteazul 2, Suceava

Constructed between 1514 and 1522 under Bogdan III and Ştefăniță Vodă, its St. George Church is inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list as one of the painted churches of Moldavia.

Parcul Şipote & Statuia lui Ştefan cel Mare — Suceava

Unveiled in 1977 near Cetatea de Scaun, the bronze equestrian monument sculpted by Iftimie Bârleanu looks out across the wooded slopes of Parcul Şipote and the Suceava skyline.
